# Pricing & Rate Card — Keffini Creative Studio

Official rate card and pricing packages for creative design, social media management, brand identity, and software development services offered by Keffini Creative Studio (Nairobi, Kenya).

## Social Media & Content Management Plans

### Starter Plan
- **Price**: KES 40,000 / month
- **Platforms Covered**: 2 platforms (e.g. Instagram & Facebook or LinkedIn)
- **Content Deliverables**: 20 custom graphics/posts per month
- **Included Features**: Professional copywriting, scheduled publishing, hashtag strategy, monthly performance recap
- **Best For**: Early-stage businesses and boutique shops building consistent digital visibility

### Growth Plan
- **Price**: KES 50,000 / month
- **Platforms Covered**: 3 platforms (e.g. Instagram, TikTok, Facebook/LinkedIn)
- **Content Deliverables**: 30 custom posts & reels per month
- **Included Features**: Motion graphics/short reels, ad campaign creatives, monthly content calendar, active audience engagement
- **Best For**: Growing brands seeking audience growth, higher conversions, and brand resonance

### Pro Plan
- **Price**: KES 80,000 / month
- **Platforms Covered**: 4 platforms (Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Facebook, X)
- **Content Deliverables**: Daily posting (40+ assets/month including motion, carousel deep-dives, stories)
- **Included Features**: Comprehensive monthly strategy, dedicated creative director, paid ad campaign management, custom photography/motion editing, community management
- **Best For**: Established businesses requiring market leadership and full-funnel creative execution

- **Turnaround**: 4 to 10 weeks
